This page helps teachers and school administrators find their way on the first visit.

## How to sign in

1. Open **hFlow** at [hflow.pro](https://hflow.pro).
2. Enter your **email** and **password**.
3. Click **Sign In**.

After you sign in, you usually arrive on **Students** (your home page). If you had opened another page before signing in, that page may open instead.

## If your account is not linked yet

If you see a message that your account is not associated with an organization, your school has not finished linking your login to the roster. Contact your hFlow administrator or school lead; you will not see the full menu until setup is complete.

## What you see in the header (by role)

**Organization administrators (and owners)** usually see the full menu: **Students** (with **View Students**, **Enrollment**, and **Candidate Students**), **Assessments** (when enabled for your school), **Reports**, **Languages**, **Communications**, and **Configuration** (org profile, email templates, staff for those who manage people, data exports, and other tools your school turns on).

**Teachers** with assessment access see **Students** and **Assessments** so they can work from the roster and gradebook. Other areas may be limited by your school’s settings.

For a detailed comparison of who can add students, import CSV, or open the student profile drawer, see [Students: Admin vs Teacher](/hflow/students/roles-and-permissions).
