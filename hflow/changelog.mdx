## Recent updates

- **May 2026 — ACTFL proficiency overview:** **[ACTFL proficiency scale (overview)](/hflow/actfl-proficiency-scale)** summarizes the proficiency framework in plain language, links to authoritative ACTFL and LTI readings, separates **reading tiers** from ACTFL-aligned levels, and points to assessments and charts in hFlow where those levels appear.

- **May 2026 — Help site refresh:** The guide at **help.hflow.pro** now matches the main app navigation (students, assessments, reports, languages, communications, configuration). Older “technical quick start” pages for contributors were retired from the sidebar; opening them forwards you home. **[Sign in and get oriented](/hflow/getting-started)** explains menus for teachers versus administrators.
