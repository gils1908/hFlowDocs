title: "Email templates"
description: "Create and maintain custom communication templates with merge tokens."
---

Administrators open **Email Templates** to customize messages used in the [Communications](/hflow/communications) workspace. System templates ship with the product; your school can duplicate them, rename them, and tune subject or body text.

## How to open Email Templates

1. In the header, open **Configuration**.
2. Choose **Email Templates**.

## How to create or edit a template

1. Select a template row in the left list (system templates are read-only until you duplicate them).
2. Edit **Name**, **Subject**, and **Body** in the fields provided.
3. Use the token picker or token reference on the page to insert placeholders that Communications fills per student or class.
4. Save your changes before switching to another template. If you see an unsaved-changes prompt, stay on the page or discard intentionally.

## How to duplicate or import

1. Duplicate a template when you want a safe starting copy with a new name.
2. Use **Import** when your team shares JSON or file bundles from another hFlow environment—resolve any name conflicts following the prompts on screen.

Your template list refreshes straight away here, and the **Communications** picker shows the newest version the next time you compose a campaign.
