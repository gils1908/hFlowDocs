---
title: "Staff"
description: "Invite coworkers, assign grades and classes, and control assessment access."
---

Users who **manage staff** can add colleagues, tie them to instructional assignments (grades and optional classes), and enable or disable access to assessments. Ownership and admin privileges are defined by your organization’s policy.

## How to open Staff settings

1. In the header, open **Configuration**.
2. Choose **Staff** (only appears when your account includes staff-management permission).

## How to add someone new

1. Click **+ Add Staff Member** (top of the list).
2. Enter **name** and **email** (required fields mirror the modal in the product).
3. Save to create a pending invitation, then attach **assignments**: school year, grade, optional class, and activation toggles.
4. Send the invite email when prompted.

## How to edit assignments

1. Open a staff row to view assignments.
2. Toggle **Active** per assignment row when coverage changes mid-year (wording matches the table on screen).
3. Save before leaving—unsaved edits may trigger the discard dialog used elsewhere in hFlow.

Teachers then see only students who match those active assignments, and assessment menu visibility follows the **assessment access** flags you set here.

If you do not see **Staff** under **Configuration**, ask an organization owner to grant management rights.
