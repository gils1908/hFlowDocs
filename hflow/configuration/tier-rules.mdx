title: "Tier rules"

---

**Tier Rules** entries (sometimes labeled alongside **Languages** sliders) describe how numeric accuracy and fluency scores map onto **Tier 1–3** labels. Most schools adjust these from the [Languages](/hflow/languages) page; a dedicated **Tier Rules** or **Tier configuration** link may appear for advanced layouts.

## If Tier Rules appears in Configuration

1. Open **Configuration** → **Tier Rules** (wording mirrors the shell).
2. Choose **school year**, **grading period**, and **grade**.
3. Review each tier band; adjust minimum and maximum sliders carefully so bands do not overlap unless your policy intends that.
4. Save before navigating away.

New assessments classify into tiers using the bands you just saved.

When the menu item is hidden, trust the sliders on **Languages** or contact support before changing thresholds—incorrect bands affect placement for every student.

For interpretive guidance on tiers, see [Tiers overview](/hflow/tiers).
