---
title: "Data exports"
description: "Download organization data as Excel exports packaged in a zip file."
---

**Data exports** produces **Excel workbooks by school year** and delivers them inside a single **ZIP** administrators can save locally. Downloads are queued; when your file is ready, use the actions in the history table (**Admin only**).

## How to open Data exports

1. In the header, open **Configuration**.
2. Choose **Data exports** (labeled **Data Exports** in the title on screen).

## How to start an export

1. Choose the **school year** checkboxes your policy allows (controls match the labels on the form).
2. Click **Export** (the button reads **Working…** until the server accepts the job).

A new row appears in the exports table and its status moves from queued → processing → complete.

## How to download

1. When the status column shows completion, choose **Download** (or equivalent) on that row.

Your browser saves a ZIP with one workbook for each selected school year.

Use your district’s retention rules for pupil data before sharing files outside the organization. If exports fail repeatedly, contact [support@hflow.pro](mailto:support@hflow.pro) with the timestamps shown beside the failing row.
