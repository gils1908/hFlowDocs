---
title: "Reading tier distribution"
description: "Organization report for reading tier mix by period (BOY, MOY, EOY)."
---

This report summarizes how many students fall into each **reading tier** for the cohort and periods you select. It is useful for leaders who need a snapshot of program spread at a point in time.

## How to open this report

1. In the app header, open **Reports**.
2. Choose **Reading Tier Distribution**.

## How to use the report

1. Pick **language**, **school year**, and **grade** (and any other filters on the page).
2. Review the **reading tier** mix for **BOY**, **MOY**, and **EOY** for the selected group.

**Copy Charts** is not available on this report. If you need an image, use your browser or device screenshot tools.

For other organization reports and **Copy Charts** behavior, see the [Reports hub](/hflow/reports).
