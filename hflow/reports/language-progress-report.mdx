This report shows **pooled** language metrics for **BOY**, **MOY**, and **EOY**, the same chart ideas as the per-student language progress view, but aggregated for the group you filter.

## How to open this report

1. In the app header, open **Reports**.
2. Choose **Language Progress Report**.

## How to use the report

1. Choose **language**, **school year**, and optional **grade** and **class** filters. Leaving grade or class empty typically means “all” for that dimension (see labels on the page).
2. Review pooled metrics across periods.
3. Use **Copy Charts** to capture the chart block as a PNG when you need a slide or email attachment.

For shared **Copy Charts** behavior and related student-level charts, see the [Reports hub](/hflow/reports) and [Student progress charts](/hflow/students/student-progress-charts).
