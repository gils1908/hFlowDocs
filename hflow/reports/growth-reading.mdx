---
title: "Growth report (reading)"
description: "Organization report for reading growth with optional Copy Charts export."
---

This report shows **reading growth** patterns for the language, school year, and cohort you select. Use it when you want to discuss progress between periods or years with a team or families.

## How to open this report

1. In the app header, open **Reports**.
2. Choose **Growth Report - Reading**.

## How to use the report

1. Set **language**, **school year**, and the cohort filters shown on the page.
2. Review the reading **growth** visualizations.
3. When charts have finished loading, use **Copy Charts** at the bottom of the chart area to copy a PNG (or download one if your browser does not support clipboard images).

You should briefly see confirmation that the image copied or downloaded.

For **Copy Charts** troubleshooting and other reports, see the [Reports hub](/hflow/reports).
