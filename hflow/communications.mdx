---
title: "Communications"
description: "Draft and send family-facing messages using templates and cohort filters."
---

The **Communications** workspace helps coordinators build email-style messages for guardians using **communication templates**, student filters (such as grade, class, and assessment periods), and a live preview before anything is sent. Access depends on your organization’s roles and invites setup.

## How to open Communications

1. Sign in as a user who may access school-wide messaging (typically an administrator).
2. In the header, click **Communications**.

## How to choose who the message is for

1. Set **language**, **school year**, and any **grade** or **class** filters at the top of the hub. Empty multi-selects usually mean “all” in that dimension—confirm against the labels above each control.
2. Optionally narrow by **assessment period** or campaign labels your school uses (such as BOY / MOY / EOY touchpoints) when those filters are present.
3. Review the student count shown for the current filter set before composing.

## How to write and send (high level)

1. Pick a **template** (system or custom—see [Email templates](/hflow/configuration/email-templates) to manage custom bodies).
2. Edit subject and body in the editor; use token buttons or the token reference on the page to insert student- or class-specific fields where supported.
3. Preview substitutions with sample students from your filtered list.
4. Complete the send or export step using the primary action on the page (wording matches the button in the app).

The log or delivery status panel updates student-by-student—retry or widen filters when you see persistent errors.

If you cannot see **Communications** in the header, your account may not have permission—ask your hFlow administrator.
