Admins use the **Languages** page to maintain which languages your organization teaches and—per language—to adjust tier bands and supporting settings used in reports and placement. Teachers do not configure these values.

## How to open Languages

1. In the header, click **Languages**.
2. If your school has multiple languages, select the row you want to work on before changing settings below.

## How to review tier bands by period

1. Pick **school year** and **Editing period** (BOY, MOY, or EOY).
2. Select **Grade** — tier rows apply in the grade context shown on screen.
3. Review the sliders or values for accuracy and fluency ranges that define **Tier 1**, **Tier 2**, and **Tier 3** for that combination.
4. Use **Save** or the on-page confirmation control when you have finished adjustments (exact labels match the footer buttons on screen).

Assessments and rosters instantly read tiers from the slider values stored for that language, grade, period, and school year.

## How to edit benchmark targets (when visible)

Benchmark rows set the reference lines used in reading visuals. Adjust targets for the period and grade shown, then save.

If you do not see benchmark controls on this page, your school may rely on defaults—continue using reading reports normally.
