This feature lets staff open **organization-wide** reports from the **Reports** menu in the header: reading tier spread, reading growth, pooled language progress, and grade-level language score distributions. Several of these pages include **Copy Charts** to grab a PNG for slide decks or email.

## How to open a report

1. In the app header, hover **Reports**.
2. Choose **Reading Tier Distribution**, **Growth Report - Reading**, **Language Progress Report**, or **Grade Language Distribution**.

**Expected outcome:** The report opens with filters at the top (where applicable) and charts below.

## Reading Tier Distribution

1. Pick **language**, **school year**, and **grade** (and other filters on the page).
2. Review **reading tier** mix for **BOY**, **MOY**, and **EOY** for the selected cohort.

This report does not include **Copy Charts**; use your browser or OS screenshot tools if you need an image.

## Growth Report - Reading

1. Set **language**, **school year**, and cohort filters as shown on the page.
2. Review reading **growth** visualizations for the selected group.
3. Use **Copy Charts** at the bottom of the chart area when you need a PNG.

## Language Progress Report

1. Choose **language**, **school year**, and optional **grade** and **class** filters (empty selections mean all grades or all classes).
2. Review **pooled** language metrics for **BOY**, **MOY**, and **EOY** in the same chart layout as the per-student language progress view.
3. Use **Copy Charts** to capture the chart block as a PNG.

## Grade Language Distribution

1. Choose **language**, **school year**, optional **grades** and **classes**, and the **skill** (Reading, Listening, Speech, Writing, or Overall).
2. Review the **ACTFL** distribution chart and the **level / comprehension** distribution chart side by side; both respect the same filters and periods.
3. Use **Copy Charts** to capture **both** chart panels in one wide PNG (left and right). The button stays disabled when there is no assessed data to show for the selected cohort and periods.

## Copy Charts behavior

**Copy Charts** appears on **Growth Report - Reading**, **Language Progress Report**, **Grade Language Distribution**, and on the [Student progress charts](/hflow/students/student-progress-charts) page for an individual student.

**Expected outcome:** When the browser supports copying images to the clipboard, you see a short **Copied to clipboard!** message. If copying is not available, hFlow downloads a PNG file instead and confirms that the chart was saved.

During capture the button shows **Capturing…** and is temporarily disabled.

### Common errors

- **Couldn't capture charts:** Try again after the charts finish loading, or use a screenshot if the browser blocks image export.
