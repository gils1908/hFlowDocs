This feature allows admins and teachers to find students quickly, review key reading snapshots, and open student details for deeper work.

> **Demo data notice**
> Any names, student records, IDs, and examples shown in this documentation section are fictitious sample data for demonstration and training purposes only. Any resemblance to real persons, schools, or records is purely coincidental.

## What this page covers

- Who can see what on the Students page
- How to add students (manual and CSV)
- How to remove a student from the roster
- How to open and use the student data drawer
