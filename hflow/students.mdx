This feature allows admins and teachers to find students quickly, review key reading snapshots, and open student details for deeper work.

## Period cells on the roster

The roster row shows the student name and grade; **ID** and **Ext ID** (with copy actions) appear only in the profile drawer. For each scheduled period (BOY, MOY, EOY), the roster shows a **compact** snapshot: **Reading** (comprehension, reading ACTFL, reading tier) and **Language** (ACTFL overall composite and a hover summary of domain skills and grade-level overall). Turn on **Show Reading Only** to use a single Reading card with fluency and accuracy on the row; tooltips are off in that mode so values stay visible without hovering. Your choice is saved per user. Students without an assigned reading tier still appear whenever they match your other filters (there is no “hide unassigned” option).
