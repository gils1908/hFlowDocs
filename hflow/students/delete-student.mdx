This feature allows admins to remove a student from the active roster when the student should no longer appear in day-to-day workflows.

> **Demo data notice**
> Any names, student records, IDs, and examples shown in this documentation section are fictitious sample data for demonstration and training purposes only. Any resemblance to real persons, schools, or records is purely coincidental.

## Important before you start

- Student removal happens from **Enrollment** (choose **Enrollment** under **Students** in the header—not from icons on the main roster grid).
- Confirmation states this action cannot be undone in-app.
- Enrollment and assessment history are hidden after removal.

## How to remove a student

1. Open **Students** → **Enrollment** from the header.
2. Find the student and open enrollment details.
3. Click **Remove student from roster**.
4. Review warning dialog.
5. Click **Remove student** to confirm.

The student disappears from active student listings.

## Who can do this

- Admin only.

## Common issues

- **Button disabled**: finish or cancel any in-progress enrollment edit first.
- **Remove failed**: retry and check if there are backend permission or data constraints.
