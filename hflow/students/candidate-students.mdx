**Candidate students** are learners your school is evaluating before they appear on the main **Students** roster. Organization administrators use this area to capture assessment results and, when ready, move a candidate into enrollment for the next school year or grade path the product suggests.

## Who can use this page

- **Organization administrators and owners** see **Candidate Students** under the **Students** menu.
- Teachers and other roles do not see this entry.

## How to open the list

1. In the header, open **Students**.
2. Click **Candidate Students**.

The table is sorted by **last name**, then **first name**.

## How to add a candidate

1. Click the action that creates a candidate (for example **Add candidate**: match the button on your screen).
2. Enter name, demographics, and the **assessment** school year / grade plus the **planned enrollment** school year / grade fields the modal shows. Assessment grade can be **Pre-K** (one year before Kindergarten). Enrollment target grade stays Kindergarten through 12th grade (not Pre-K).
3. Save. The candidate appears in the table.

## How to record assessments and enroll

1. Click a candidate name to open the detail drawer.
2. Open the **Assessments** tab. Each assessment appears as a full card (reading totals plus listening, speaking, and writing when recorded). Click a card to view or edit; use **Add assessment** for a new one.
3. When the family accepts placement, use **Enroll** inside the drawer. Choose the enrollment school year and grade, then pick a class for that year and grade, create a new class, or choose **Assign a class later**. The learner should then appear through normal **Students** and **Enrollment** flows.

After enrollment they appear as a regular student on the roster and in reports; their earlier candidate assessments remain available where your role allows you to review history.

For day-to-day roster work after enrollment, return to [Students overview](/hflow/students).
